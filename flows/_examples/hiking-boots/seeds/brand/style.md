# North Notch — Brand Guide

LOCKED. Restate verbatim inside every Fal prompt.

## Identity
- Brand: North Notch
- Product: indie hiking boots, made in small Vermont batches
- Target audience: weekend hikers in their 30s-40s who treat their gear like heirlooms, not status objects

## Voice & Tone
earnest, slightly off-kilter, documentary — never cynical, never bro-y, never "crushing it"

## Visual Bible
16mm film, naturalistic light, hands-and-laces close-ups. Pacific Northwest / Northeast hardwoods, fog, wet stone. Muted forest palette with one warm leather highlight. Documentary stillness — NOT triumphant orchestral, NOT slow-motion hero shots, NOT mountain-vista wide-angles. Real feet, real mud, real wear. 16:9.
