# Banned Terms and Imagery

## Do not use these words in any prompt or copy
- luxury
- premium
- revolutionary
- game-changing
- epic
- amazing
- incredible
- world-class
- next-level

## Do not generate
- Implied or explicit violence
- Identifiable faces of real public figures
- Copyrighted logos or brand marks not owned by the client
- Stock-photo-style posed group shots with fake smiles
- AI-obvious artefacts presented as finished creative (melting fingers, garbled text)
- High-key white-background product-only shots (product must be in context)

## Sensitive categories (avoid unless explicitly briefed)
- Alcohol consumption
- Extreme sports without safety gear shown
- Environmental damage imagery (e.g., single-use plastics)
