# Brand Style Guide

## Tone
- Confident, human, slightly irreverent
- Never corporate, never generic stock-photo energy
- Action and texture over perfection

## Visual Style
- Natural light preferred; golden hour, overcast outdoor light
- Colour palette: earthy neutrals (sand, slate, pine), accent with burnt orange or rust
- Grain and texture welcome — avoid over-processed, hyper-saturated looks
- People in motion or mid-task, not posed

## Shot Composition
- Widescreen 16:9 for all shots
- Lead with the product in context (on feet, in hand, in environment) not isolated on white
- Environment should reinforce the product story

## Copy / Supers (if applicable)
- Short, punchy — 5 words or fewer per super
- Title case, no exclamation marks
- No buzzwords: "premium", "luxury", "revolutionary", "game-changing"

## Pacing (for motion)
- 6s clips default; cut on motion, not on stillness
- First frame must hook in under 1s
